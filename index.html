👤 Biography

Hi there👋, my name is Zhirui Gao (in Chinese: 高志锐).

I received my Ph.D. degree from National University of Defense Technology (2021–2026), supervised by Prof. Kai Xu, Prof. Wei Chen, and Assoc. Prof. Renjiao Yi. Prior to that, I earned my Bachelor's degree from China University of Geosciences (2017–2021).

I am currently a researcher at Huawei focusing on Embodied AI. My research interests broadly lie in 3D Vision and Embodied Intelligence, including 3D Reconstruction, World Models, WAM, and VLA models.

I am open to collaborations and actively recruiting research interns and full-time employees. Feel free to reach out via email!

🏆 TIP 2023 · CCF-A
  • Delving into crispness: Guided label refinement for crisp edge detection
    Yunfan Ye, Renjiao Yi, Zhirui Gao, Zhiping Cai, Kai Xu

    [arXiv] [Project Page] [Code]

    • We propose an effective Canny-guided refinement of human-labeled edges whose result can be used to train crisp edge detectors. Essentially, it seeks for a subset of over-detected Canny edges that best align human labels. We show that several existing edge detectors can be turned into a crisp edge detector through training on our refined edge maps.
🏆 CVPR 2023 · CCF-A
  • NEF: Neural Edge Fields for 3D Parametric Curve Reconstruction from Multi-view Images
    Yunfan Ye, Renjiao Yi, Zhirui Gao, Chenyang Zhu, Zhiping Cai, Kai Xu

    [arXiv] [Project Page] [Code]

    • We study the problem of reconstructing 3D feature curves of an object from a set of calibrated multi-view images. To do so, we learn a neural implicit field representing the density distribution of 3D edges which we refer to as Neural Edge Field (NEF).
